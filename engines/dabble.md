## Notes

Author's site supplies Windows executables, source and MSVC++ project files. Its readme.txt explicitly sizes boards in dots: dabble.exe supports up to 6×6 dots, while dabble_nohash.exe supports up to 20×20 dots. Therefore 5×6 boxes requires the no-hash executable on a 6×7-dot board. The cited comparison is historical and does not establish current leading strength.
