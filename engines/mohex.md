## Notes

This repository is a CMake port of Benzene with development to 2015, including the parallel DFPN solver behind the 9×9 and 10×10 f5 results (Pawlewicz & Hayward 2013). The Olympiad results are for 11×11 and 13×13 play, not the solved board sizes.
